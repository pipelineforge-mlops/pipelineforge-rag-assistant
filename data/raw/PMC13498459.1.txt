Correction to: Nature Cancer 10.1038/s43018-026-01199-y, published online 1 July 2026.

In the version of the article initially published, in the Abstract, the sentence “The 8-year progression-free and overall survival (OS) rates were 0.42 months (confidence interval (CI): 0.24–0.59) and 0.66 months (CI: 0.46–0.79), respectively” should have read “The 8-year progression-free and overall survival (OS) rates were 0.42 (confidence interval (CI): 0.24–0.59) and 0.66 (CI: 0.46–0.79), respectively”. This correction has been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
