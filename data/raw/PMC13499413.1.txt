==============================
This article presents AryWiki-Instruct, a high-fidelity instruction tuning dataset for Moroccan Arabic (Darija), comprising 46,590 Question and Answer (QA) pairs. The dataset was derived from a snapshot of the Moroccan Arabic Wikipedia (arywiki) and generated using the Gemini-2.5-Flash model via a Context Aware batch processing architecture. The data creation process involved parsing raw XML Wikipedia dumps, filtering for script consistency and token density, and applying a Context Injection generation strategy to prevent coreference ambiguity. To ensure high information density, the raw generated output (82,500 pairs) was subjected to a rigorous automated quality assurance pipeline. This pipeline utilized a hierarchical trigger confirmation algorithm to remove repetitive administrative census noise and employed composite embedding based clustering (multilingual-e5-large) for semantic deduplication. The final dataset is formatted as a JSONL file, providing paired instructions and responses alongside their taxonomic categories. This dataset provides a native first culturally grounded resource designed to facilitate the supervised fine tuning of Large Language Models (LLMs) in Maghrebi dialects, circumventing the syntactic limitations of translated English instruction sets.

1 Value of the Data

• The dataset addresses the critical resource scarcity and orthographic ambiguity in North African dialects, providing a high-quality dense corpus specifically engineered for Moroccan Darija.

• By utilizing native Moroccan Wikipedia articles rather than translating existing English instruction sets (e.g., Alpaca), the data preserves authentic dialectal morphology and cultural context, preventing the translation artifacts commonly found in LLM fine tuning.

• Natural Language Processing (NLP) researchers and engineers can reuse this JSONL dataset to execute Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT) or Parameter Efficient Fine Tuning (PEFT) on open weight foundation models to improve their reasoning and generation capabilities in Maghrebi dialects.

• The provided algorithmic methodology, specifically the hierarchical filtration logic and composite embedding deduplication, serves as a replicable framework for researchers aiming to generate and purify instruction data for other low resource vernaculars suffering from high administrative stub noise.

• A qualitative comparison demonstrates the morphological superiority of authentic native generation over machine translated datasets. As detailed in Table 3, existing resources (such as translated Alpaca [2]) frequently exhibit translation artifacts, including rigid English prompting wrappers, literal idiom translations, and fallback to Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). AryWiki-Instruct circumvents these issues by utilizing native Moroccan interrogatives and localized vocabulary.

2 Background

The adaptation of Large Language Models (LLMs) to North African vernaculars, particularly Moroccan Arabic (Darija), is severely bottlenecked by a scarcity of high-quality digital resources. While contemporary open-weight models demonstrate high proficiency in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), they frequently suffer from “catastrophic forgetting” or revert to high-resource pivot languages (such as English or MSA) when processing Maghrebi dialects. Existing methodologies often rely on the direct machine translation of English instruction sets, which introduces unnatural translation artifacts and fails to capture dialect-specific morpho-syntax. The motivation behind AryWiki-Instruct is to address this representation gap by providing a dense, native-first instruction tuning corpus. By deriving factual Question and Answer pairs directly from the Moroccan Arabic Wikipedia, this dataset ensures that the resulting language models are grounded in authentic dialectal morphology, local geography, and cultural context.

The disproportionate reduction in the GEOGRAPHY domain (97.3% removal rate) was a deliberate design choice implemented to prevent the propagation of structural biases inherent in the source material. The Moroccan Wikipedia contains tens of thousands of automated, template based geographical stubs (e.g., “X is a village in Y province with Z population”). Retaining these stubs would severely skew the dataset distribution, conditioning downstream models to overfit on rigid, low entropy syntactic templates rather than learning generalized dialectal reasoning. By aggressively pruning this template heavy domain while preserving narrative rich domains (such as BIOGRAPHY and CULTURE), the residual distribution ensures that the dataset promotes diverse semantic reasoning over repetitive factoid memorization.

4 Experimental Design, Materials and Methods

4.1 Foundational corpus construction and preprocessing

The primary textual stratum of the dataset was derived from a January 2026 XML dump of the Moroccan Arabic Wikipedia (arywiki). The raw MediaWiki markup was parsed and excised utilizing the wikiextractor tool [5]. Because low resource Wikipedia distributions frequently contain low information artifacts and empty taxonomy templates, a heuristic thresholding step was applied. Document vectors containing fewer than 80 characters or fewer than 10 tokens were systematically discarded to establish a rigorous baseline of information density.

To ensure script consistency and prevent tokenization fragmentation, a typological inclusion filter targeting the Arabic Unicode block ([\u0600 \u06FF]) was enforced. Subsequently, morphological standardization was applied to address graphemic variance in Moroccan Darija. A grapheme unification strategy mapped all Alef variants to the unhamzated form, and Western punctuation marks were systematically aligned to their Arabic Unicode counterparts to facilitate accurate sentence boundary detection (Fig. 4).Fig. 4 The corpus curation pipeline: the sequential application of filters ensures that computationally expensive normalization is performed only on valid text rich articles.

4.2 Context aware generation

To bridge the modality gap between static Wikipedia articles and conversational data, a multi stage generation pipeline was engineered utilizing the Gemini 2.5 Flash model. A Context Injection strategy was implemented to resolve coreference ambiguity during the generation process.

For a source document D segmented into a sequence of semantic chunks {C1,C2,…,Cn}, the generation prompt Pi for a specific chunk Ci was constructed as:(1) Pi=Isys⊕S(D)⊕Ci

Where Isys represents the system instruction, S(D) denotes a global document summary generated in an initial preprocessing pass, and the ⊕ operator denotes string concatenation. This global context was utilized for entity disambiguation, while the local chunk Ci served as the strict grounding source for answer extraction to minimize hallucinations (Fig. 5).Fig. 5 The context injection architecture: separating the input into a global summary for entity grounding and a local chunk for strict fact extraction.

Fig 5 dummy alt text

To maximize data yield, a hierarchical segmentation and batch extraction methodology was applied. The expansion process is modeled as a multiplicative function of segmentation granularity and extraction density. Let Draw represent the total volume of the raw generated instruction corpus:(2) Draw=∑i=1Ndocs∑j=1Mi|G(Cij)|

Where Ndocs denotes the total number of filtered source articles, Mi represents the number of valid semantic chunks derived from article i, Cij refers to the j-th chunk of article i, and G(Cij) is the generative extraction function returning the set of unique Question and Answer pairs derived from that chunk. The |·| operator denotes the cardinality (total number of elements) of the returned set.

To ensure taxonomic consistency and prevent semantic overlap, the 11 taxonomic tags were assigned deterministically using a Document Level Fixed Taxonomy mechanism. We predefined a closed list of 11 macro domains representing standard encyclopedic distributions used by Wikipedia. Prior to extracting individual Question and Answer pairs, an initial API call was executed on the entire source article, prompting the model to select exactly one dominant tag from the predefined list that best described the global document. This selected tag was subsequently propagated to all derived local semantic chunks. This approach prevented stochastic tagging variance (for example, erroneously tagging one paragraph about a stadium as SPORTS and another as GEOGRAPHY), ensuring high cluster coherence. A safety fallback was implemented in the extraction script to automatically assign the GENERAL tag if the model hallucinated a category outside the predefined list (Fig. 6).Fig. 6 The Optimized pipeline architecture decoupling high level semantic analysis from granular fact extraction.

4.3 Automated quality assurance and semantic refinement

A rigorous post generation audit was required to address a distributional skew caused by automated Wikipedia census stubs. A Hierarchical Priority Logic was engineered to distinguish between high value macro administrative entities and low entropy micro administrative boilerplate, as detailed in Algorithm 1.Algorithm 1 Hierarchical Trigger Confirmation Filtration

if not IsProtected then
return False // Discard Statistical Boilerplate
end
end

Furthermore, a boundary aware regular expression matching strategy was implemented to handle the concatenative morphology of Darija. This filter explicitly permitted a closed set of valid Darija proclitics (such as conjunctions and prepositions) to attach to word boundaries, targeting specific semantic instances without purging high quality questions containing morphologically similar but distinct roots.

Following heuristic filtration, a semantic clustering pipeline was executed to detect and remove semantically isomorphic pairs. The pipeline leveraged the intfloat/multilingual-e5-large dense retriever. To resolve ambiguity in short dialectal queries, a composite embedding strategy was adopted. For a pair Pi, the vector representation vi∈R1024 was computed by embedding the concatenation of the instruction qi and the response ai:(3) vi=Eθ(qi⊕ai)

Where Eθ represents the dense retrieval embedding function parameterized by the model weights θ, and the ⊕ operator denotes string concatenation.

A pairwise Cosine Similarity matrix was computed for all candidates generated from the same source document. Pairs exceeding a similarity threshold of α=0.94 were flagged as redundant. In such cases, the algorithm retained the candidate featuring the more comprehensive response length. A final topology guided pruning pass increased the similarity strictness to α=0.85 exclusively for the Geography domain to isolate and remove residual structural templates.

4.4 Human evaluation protocol

To validate the linguistic acceptability and factual grounding of the final corpus, a double-blind Human in the Loop evaluation campaign was conducted. A stratified random sample of N=196 instruction and response triplets was extracted from the final dataset. The evaluation was distributed among two native Darija linguists operating in disjoint batches to prevent source identification.

Evaluators rated the instructions and responses on a 5-point Likert scale across three orthogonal dimensions: Morphosyntactic Authenticity (Grammar), Factual Adherence (Factuality), and Instruction Following (Adherence). Limitations

While the human evaluation protocol validates the dataset's linguistic acceptability and factual grounding, the sample size of 196 inspected inputs is relatively small compared to the total corpus volume of 46,590 pairs. Expanding the number of human-inspected records would provide a more robust statistical guarantee of overall dataset quality. However, the primary objective of this methodology was to engineer a fully automated pipeline for extracting and curating high-fidelity instruction datasets from raw Wikipedia dumps. The reliance on algorithmic heuristics and semantic embedding filters rather than extensive manual intervention, is a deliberate design choice intended to ensure that the dataset generation process remains highly scalable and easily reproducible for other low-resource dialects.
